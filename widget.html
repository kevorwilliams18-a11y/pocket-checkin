Pocket Short Cakes widget

Put Pickles and your daily note on your iPhone Home Screen.

  1. Install Scriptable, then tap Copy widget script below.
  2. In Scriptable, tap +, paste the script, and name it Pocket Short Cakes. Tap Done.
  3. Add a Scriptable widget to your Home Screen. Choose medium to show the full note.
  4. Press and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, then select Pocket Short Cakes under Script.
